UC San Diego MS Computer Science is an in-person program located in La Jolla, California, with strong industry ties to San Diego's biotech and tech sectors as well as proximity to Silicon Valley companies. The program is ranked in the top 15-25 nationally. Total tuition is approximately $37,000 for California residents and slightly higher for out-of-state students; this reflects the full program cost. The GRE is optional for admission starting in recent admissions cycles. Specializations include systems, machine learning, networking, and human-computer interaction. The program takes approximately 18 months to complete. UCSD's International Students and Programs Office provides visa support for F-1 students. The graduation rate is approximately 85%. The application fee is $120. UCSD is known for cutting-edge systems research through the Systems and Networking group and collaborations with Qualcomm, Intel, and biotech companies in the San Diego area. The program is an excellent value relative to peer institutions on the East Coast.
