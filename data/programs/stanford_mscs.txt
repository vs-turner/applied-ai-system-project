Stanford University MS Computer Science is one of the most competitive and prestigious graduate programs in the world, consistently ranked in the top 3 globally. The program is located on campus in Stanford, California, near Silicon Valley, giving students direct access to tech industry leaders, startups, and research labs. Total tuition is approximately $67,000 for the full program. The GRE is required for admission. Students can specialize in areas including machine learning, artificial intelligence, systems, and theoretical computer science. The program typically takes 18 months to complete for full-time students. Stanford offers strong visa and immigration support services for international students through the Bechtel International Center. The graduation rate is approximately 95%. Faculty include world-renowned researchers in AI, ML, and systems who regularly collaborate with companies like Google, Meta, and Apple. Students have access to the Stanford AI Lab (SAIL) and other cutting-edge research facilities. The application fee is $125. Admission is extremely competitive with acceptance rates below 5%.
