Columbia University MS Computer Science is an in-person program located in New York City, offering students unmatched access to the finance, tech, and media industries concentrated in Manhattan. The program is ranked in the top 25 CS graduate programs nationally. Total tuition is approximately $65,000. The GRE is required for admission. Specializations include machine learning, computer systems, computer security, and data science. The program typically takes 18 months to complete. Columbia has a dedicated International Students and Scholars Office that provides visa and immigration support for F-1 and J-1 students. The graduation rate is approximately 88%. The application fee is $85. Columbia's location in NYC gives students access to internships and full-time positions at companies including Goldman Sachs, Google NYC, and numerous startups. Students can participate in research through Columbia's Data Science Institute and the Columbia Artificial Intelligence (ColumbiaAI) group.
