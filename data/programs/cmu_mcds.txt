Carnegie Mellon University MS Computational Data Science (MCDS) is a specialized master's program focused on large-scale data systems, machine learning pipelines, and data analytics at scale. The program is located in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and is backed by CMU's top-1 ranked School of Computer Science. Total tuition is approximately $73,000, making it one of the more expensive programs. The GRE is not required for admission, though it is highly recommended. Specializations include data science, machine learning, and systems with a data engineering focus. The program takes 16 months to complete as a focused, intensive degree. CMU provides strong visa and international student support services. The graduation rate is approximately 93%. The standard application fee is $100. MCDS is specifically designed for students targeting data engineering, ML infrastructure, and applied data science roles rather than pure research. Graduates are placed at companies including Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and data-intensive financial firms. The program's industry partnerships and CMU brand command top salaries in the data science job market.
