Arizona State University MS Computer Science is a fully online program delivered through ASU Online, one of the largest online degree programs in the United States. The program is flexible and designed for working professionals and career changers. Total tuition is approximately $15,000 for the full program, making it one of the most affordable options available. No GRE is required for admission. Specializations include software engineering, systems, and human-computer interaction. The program can take up to 30 months to complete depending on the student's pace. As a fully online program, ASU does not offer on-campus visa support. The graduation rate is approximately 71%. The application fee is $70. ASU Online's large student body and established infrastructure make it a reliable choice for students who need flexibility. The program is accredited and recognized by employers, though it does not carry the same prestige as top-25 programs. Ideal for students prioritizing low cost, schedule flexibility, and no GRE requirement over brand recognition.
