Georgia Institute of Technology Online MS Computer Science (OMSCS) is one of the most affordable and accessible accredited CS master's programs available. The program is delivered fully online, allowing students to complete coursework from anywhere in the world. Total tuition is approximately $7,000 for the full program, making it exceptionally budget-friendly. No GRE is required for admission. Specializations include machine learning, computing systems, software engineering, and human-computer interaction. The program is self-paced and typically takes 24 to 36 months to complete, though motivated students can finish faster. The graduation rate is approximately 72%. OMSCS does not provide on-campus visa support since it is a fully remote program; international students should verify local regulations. The application fee is $75. The program is backed by Georgia Tech's strong reputation and the same curriculum as the on-campus program. Class sizes are large but instruction quality is consistently praised. Ideal for working professionals or students on a tight budget.
