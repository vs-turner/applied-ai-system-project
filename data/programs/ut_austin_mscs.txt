University of Texas at Austin MS Computer Science is an in-person program in Austin, Texas, ranked consistently in the top 15-25 CS graduate programs nationally. Austin is a growing major tech hub with companies including Dell, Oracle, Apple, and many startups. Total tuition is approximately $20,000 for the full program, making it one of the most affordable in-person top-25 programs available. The GRE is not required for admission. Specializations include machine learning, systems, computer security, and software engineering. The program takes approximately 18 months to complete. UT Austin provides international student services and F-1 visa support through the International Student and Scholar Services office. The graduation rate is approximately 87%. The application fee is $65 for U.S.-based applicants and $90 for international applicants. UT Austin's UTCS department is known for strong systems and security research, and graduates are highly sought after in industry. The combination of low tuition and strong rankings makes this a highly competitive choice for budget-conscious students.
