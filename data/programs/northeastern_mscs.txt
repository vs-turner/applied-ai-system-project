Northeastern University MS Computer Science is a hybrid program based in Boston, Massachusetts, with campuses in multiple cities including Seattle and San Francisco. The program is ranked in the top 50 CS graduate programs and is known for its co-op program that integrates six-month industry internships into the degree. Total tuition is approximately $42,000 for the full program. No GRE is required for admission. Specializations include machine learning, software engineering, human-computer interaction, and data science. The program typically takes 24 months to complete, extended if co-op placements are pursued. Northeastern's Global Student Success team provides visa and immigration support for international students. The graduation rate is approximately 83%. The application fee is $100. Northeastern's co-op program distinguishes it from pure research programs; students often graduate with 6-12 months of professional industry experience. Boston's tech ecosystem includes biotech companies, financial firms, and divisions of Google and Amazon. Hybrid delivery offers flexibility for part-time and remote learners.
