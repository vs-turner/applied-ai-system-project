University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign Master of Computer Science (MCS) is a fully online, non-thesis professional degree designed for students seeking industry careers. The program is ranked among the top 5 CS programs in the US and is delivered entirely online, allowing remote completion. Total tuition is approximately $22,000 for the full program. No GRE is required for admission. Specialization areas include machine learning, systems, and data science. The program typically takes 24 months to complete for students taking two courses per semester. The graduation rate is approximately 80%. UIUC MCS does not offer on-campus visa support as it is a remote program. The application fee is $70. The UIUC CS department is known for its strong systems and programming languages research, and the department is consistently ranked in the top tier. Students gain access to UIUC's alumni network, which includes many senior engineers and researchers at major tech companies. The MCS degree is valued by employers including Microsoft, Amazon, and Google.
