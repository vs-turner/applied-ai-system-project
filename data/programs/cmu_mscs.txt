Carnegie Mellon University MS Computer Science is a top-ranked program known for its rigorous curriculum and strong placement in both research and industry. The program is based in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and is consistently ranked among the top 5 CS graduate programs worldwide. Total tuition is approximately $58,000. The GRE is not required for admission, though strong scores can still help strengthen an application. Specialization tracks include machine learning, systems, computer security, and robotics. The program typically completes in 16 months for full-time students. CMU has dedicated international student support services and strong visa assistance. The graduation rate is approximately 94%. CMU's proximity to major tech employers in Pittsburgh and strong alumni network in Silicon Valley provides excellent career placement. The application fee is $100. Students can collaborate with CMU's CyLab security institute, the Robotics Institute, and the Machine Learning Department. Admission is highly competitive.
